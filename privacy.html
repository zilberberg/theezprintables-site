Privacy Policy

TheEZPrintables Manager · Last updated: October 27, 2026

TheEZPrintables Manager is a personal tool used by the owner of the Etsy shop TheEZPrintables to manage that shop's own listings and its own Pinterest account. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What data it accesses

  • Pinterest: the owner's own boards, pins and pin analytics (impressions, saves, clicks), in order to publish pins for the shop's products and see how they perform.
  • Etsy: the owner's own shop listings and sales records, in order to create and update listings and see which products sell.

It does not access other Pinterest users' or Etsy shops' data.

How data is stored

The tool runs on the owner's own computer. Access tokens and any data it retrieves are stored locally on that computer only. Nothing is sent to any third-party server other than the Pinterest and Etsy APIs themselves.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared or disclosed to anyone. There are no analytics, advertising or tracking services in the tool.

Buyers

Purchases happen on Etsy, under Etsy's privacy policy. The tool only reads the shop's own order records to count sales per product. It does not contact buyers.

Deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time in Pinterest's and Etsy's account settings. Locally stored data is deleted by removing it from the owner's computer.
